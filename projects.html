Projects

PATRIOT

Built PATRIOT, an emergency and news reporting app that connects Ghanaian citizens directly to responders such as the Ghana Police Service and ambulance services. With this idea and build, we took second place in the DCS UI/UX hackathon.

KROMTHRIFT

Kromthrift is a side business I run amongst other endeavours: an online thrift store selling all kinds of backpacks. I designed the webpage for it.

AUTOMATIC RELAY

As a computer engineer, I built an automatic power relay system with my team for our level 100 computer innovations project. It switches over to a battery charged by solar panels whenever mains power cuts out.

MOBILE PHONE DETECTOR

For another course project, I built a mobile phone detector. It works by matching the radio frequency of a phone as it transmits or receives information, using an antenna I designed. When resonance is achieved, a buzzer sounds to signal that a phone has been detected.
